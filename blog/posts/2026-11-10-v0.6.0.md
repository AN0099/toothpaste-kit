Version 0.6.0 is a MINOR release under `docs/releasing.md`. Review rounds, a bill of materials,
SPDX provenance, a Security Insights file, the kit blog and two multi-architecture containers
arrive. `scripts/gates.sh` goes from fourteen gates to twenty-one, and each Python script the gates
selftest runs now holds a coverage floor. No skill is added, and no skill revision is BREAKING.

## Review rounds

A review round has three JSON schemas in `orchestration/schemas/round/`, for the packet, the
reviewer's report and the operator's decision. `scripts/round-check.py` validates a round directory
against them and checks the invariants that span the records: a reviewer never verifies their own
finding, the operator cites only reproduced findings, and the reviewed revision matches the packet's.
`examples/review-round/` holds a practice fixture with an answer key, and `tests/e2e/` runs the
fixture rounds as an end-to-end entry point. `docs/review-rounds.md` is the guide, and
`docs/review-rounds-quickstart.md` the short path.

## Provenance and a bill of materials

Commits now record provenance as `Assisted-by:` and `Agent-Effort:` trailers, and every commit needs
a `Signed-off-by:` line. `scripts/spdx-fragment.py` renders a commit range's trailers as one SPDX
3.0.1 JSON-LD document. `scripts/sbom.py` prints an SPDX 3.0.1 bill of materials for the tree, and
exits 1 on a file with no licence or copyright, a licence with no text under `LICENSES/`, or an
action not pinned by commit SHA, apart from the one exception `docs/security-posture.md` records.
`security-insights.yml` describes the kit's security arrangements in a form tools read.

## Two containers

`containers/verify/` holds every third-party checker the kit's checks call, each at an exact version
and each download checked by sha256, so no check is skipped and nothing is installed on the host.
`containers/starter/` is a way to try the kit without installing its dependencies. Both build for
linux/amd64 and linux/arm64, and `.github/workflows/containers.yml` builds each image on a native
runner for each architecture and runs the gates selftest inside it.

## The gates

Seven gates arrive, among them the review-round records, the bill of materials and the blog
builder's selftest. A coverage gate, `scripts/coverage-check.py`, holds each of the 13 Python scripts
the gates selftest runs at or over its floor, the measured statement coverage on adding it. Selftests
for nine scripts gained cases from mutation testing, each killing a mutant the old selftest let
through.

## Also new

- This blog: `scripts/blog.py` builds `blog/posts/*.md` into static pages and an RSS feed, and
  `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys them to [kit.bridleworks.org](https://kit.bridleworks.org/).
- `docs/installing.md` has a section on updating a fork.
- `scripts/check-qblock` checks a reply's closing question block, with 30 planted cases.
- `orchestration/protocol.md` gains a faculty-consultation format, a reply under eight plain Markdown
  headers.
- Between the cutoff and the tag, nothing merges into `main` except the release itself.

## Known accessibility limitations

`docs/accessibility.md` is a working draft and a research position. The accessibility of produced
artifacts beyond this repository's own Markdown is not covered, the blog's generated HTML and RSS
feed have had no separate accessibility check, and this release makes no accessibility conformance
or certification claim.
